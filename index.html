Critical Tables

Fortune and folly on the natural 20 and the natural 1

The blade remembers every boast you ever made.

An old sellsword, between drinks

Enter the d100 roll, then pick what happened: a critical hit or a critical fail.

How the Tables Work
  • Critical hit: a player rolls a natural 20 on an attack against an enemy. Use the row for the attack's damage type. The damage instruction (double, triple, maximum, quadruple) is the critical damage; it stands in for the usual extra dice.
  • Critical fail: anyone rolls a natural 1 on an attack. Use the row for the attack type: melee, ranged, natural (limbs, bites and the like) or magical.
  • Severity by level: the source recommends only the first band at level 1, the second from level 5, the third from level 9 and the last from level 13. For critical fails at level 1 you can halve the d100 roll to stay in Awkward.
  • Fails, once per encounter: each character or creature rolls only for its first critical miss in an encounter. If the result does not apply, it keeps rolling on later misses until one does.
  • Saves on fails: where a save is allowed to end an ongoing effect, it can be repeated free at the end of each of that creature's turns.
  • Doesn't fit? Roll again, or for a hit, just use double damage.
  • "You" means whoever rolled the 20 or the 1. Where no end point is given, the GM decides how (or whether) the effect ends.

Notes for 5.5e
  • Extra crit dice: the source has half-orcs and barbarians roll the d100 several times and pick one. In 2024 the Orc species has no Savage Attacks and Brutal Critical is gone, so this rarely comes up.
  • Surge of power (hit tables 9 and 25, Magical): recasting with a spell slot breaks the 2024 rule of one spell slot per turn. Let the card override it, or treat the second cast as a free cast like table 40.
  • Contested checks (hit 14, 30, 45; fail 45): 2024 mostly swapped contests for saves. Run them as written, or have the target make a save against DC 8 + Strength (or Dex) modifier + proficiency bonus.
  • Stunned with no end (hit 4, 20, 35): unlike the fail tables, the hit tables give no repeat save. Consider a repeat Constitution save at the end of each of the target's turns.
  • Weapon Mastery: some results overlap masteries (Vex, Slow, Topple, Sap). They simply stack.
  • Polymorph and Wild Shape (fail 50, fail 18) use the 2024 versions of those rules.
